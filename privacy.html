p@use Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

p@use is one button that shows a small surprise. It's made by That Sound (@water).

What we collect

Nothing. p@use has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your press count, the surprises you keep ("Kept"), and your settings (appearance, daily reminder time) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

p@use+ is a subscription handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel it anytime in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

New surprises

p@use may download a public list of new surprises from our server. This download does not send any information about you.

Notifications

If you turn on "Daily pause", p@use schedules one reminder a day on your phone. You can turn it off anytime in the app or in Settings.

Children

p@use doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
